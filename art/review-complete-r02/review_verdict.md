# Independent complete-art review r02

Reviewer: `/root/complete_review_r02`, independent of generation, processing and audit. No earlier review verdict or builder reasoning was consulted. This verdict covers the supplied complete-art pack and its named visual integration evidence only.

## Method and coverage

All five actor lineups, all 68 main strips, all 11 static lineups and all 36 static strips were opened first. Plain observations were saved in `blind_notes.md` and reread before scope, integration, audit or derivation metadata. Every frame shown in each strip was visually inspected. Then all 67 main onion sheets, three body/whip composites, both static onion sheets, all terrain repeat/patch/platform sheets and both background repeat/mirror-join pairs were opened. All five registered style reference images were inspected.

All 68 source-reel entries (one calibration still and 67 runtime sequences, including four supplemental hurt contexts) were played through Chromium requestAnimationFrame at the viewer’s gameplay cadence. The harness sampled the live canvas through a full duration plus endpoint margin. Every frame index was observed for every entry; eight temporal samples per entry were visually inspected alongside the source strips/onions. `playback_observations.json` records all68 entries; `playback_samples/` and `playback_harness.cjs` retain that evidence. The contact sheets are temporal samples, not a claim of continuous human video observation. Interrupted batches were rerun; only complete per-entry records are included.

All70 supplied integration images were opened:12 actual input/AI native/phone captures;15 native and15 phone fixed-stage/context captures;18 effect/UI/menu/portrait stills;8 browser captures;2 packed exit-state fixtures. These establish the depicted visual states. The packed exit fixtures intentionally isolate packed assets and are not full-scene background evidence.

## Identity, anatomy and motion

The hunter keeps the brown tied hair, mantle, leather/linen costume and booted full body. Standing/stride tiles have visible full legs; crouch folds the hips and knees and keeps a comparable head. The boss retains its crown, asymmetric gauntlets, cloth and two plate legs. The pursuer remains an elongated four-limbed wrong creature; the ranged figure is intentionally rooted to its post with a pooled robe, and the swooper intentionally has wings and cloth rather than legs. Ordinary canonical action sources face right; front-facing intermediate turn poses are intentional. No torso-only replacement, global scale drift, blank damage frame, clipped limb, key-color fringe or unexplained debris was seen.

Onions show connected limb trajectories. The walk is brisk and the boss’s upper body is restrained, but foot/leg articulation is visible. The swooper’s membrane strokes change projection around attached shoulders; recovery has stronger repeated strokes than cruise. Collapses preserve the actor before an established fade to ash. Supported get-up proceeds from palm/hip contact through a planted half-kneel; reversed crouch exit reads as standing up. All declared derived clips were compared with their intended actions after blind description. Scripted/reused derivations remain documented production quality debt, not unlabelled paint.

All three attack composites show the same fist taking the grip through wind-up, extension and recoil. The narrow leather thong stays attached, forms compact resting coils, reaches through the active yellow regions at F2/F3, and gathers during recovery. No energy-weapon glow is present. The effects expand/peak/contract or sustain the explicitly declared telegraph, remain anchored and have no rectangle border.
