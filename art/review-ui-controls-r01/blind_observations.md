# Blind observations

Viewed lineup and all six individual strips before any intent, index, audit, metadata, contracts, or briefs.

All six controls share a square dark stone/metal beveled frame and pale ivory icons. Left/right arrows are clear and symmetrical in weight; jump is an upward arrow; whip is a recognizable coiled implement; crouch is a compact bent figure; pause is two upright bars. Each strip presents two states: first has a gray/purple edge and second a visibly amber inset rim with darker center. The icon position appears stable or very slightly depressed, and the amber rim gives an obvious state cue. Edge thickness, overall footprint and visual texture are consistent across the set. Crouch has the most compact internal silhouette; whip has the finest details. No visible clipping or stray outside marks are apparent in the supplied composed views. Labels above the strips are presentation labels and not evidence of runtime word legibility.

Provisional issues to inspect in raw/runtime evidence: icon readability at small size, crouch recognition, icon contrast over dark center, transparent margins, pressed distinction when scaled, and whether words remain readable independently from the textured tiles.
