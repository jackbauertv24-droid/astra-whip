Scope: one proposed unsupported-wing death-collapse key, `art/production-candidates/swooper-death-collapse-r03/key.png`. This review does not approve any full animation, temporal continuity, ash-settle, or engine behavior.

Protocol: opened only the proposed key first, then saved `blind_observations.md` before opening identity, style-lock references, or contracts. I did not make the art and did not inspect prior rejected reviews or builder verdicts. ## Findings

- **Creature identity/material: substantially retained.** The small hooded shroud, paired ribbed membrane wings, ragged trailing cloth, muted ash/brown-gray paint, worn highlights, and dark folds belong to the approved creature and the painterly gothic style lock. There is no gore, animal/pet read, glow, or foreign costume detail.
- **Completeness and attachments: visually acceptable.** The whole shrouded figure, central trailing strips, and two wings are present. Both wing roots connect to the shoulder area. Nothing is visibly cropped, and there is no visible scenery, ground, lettering, or detached debris. The black image-viewer field alone is not evidence of an opaque backdrop; this is a visual assessment rather than a formal PNG-alpha audit.
- **Proportions: identity is recognizable, but the hood appears broader and more upright than the identity reference.** Some of the reduced overall span is credible wing articulation. It must not be treated as permission to enlarge the hood or shorten wing bones. The current pose does not provide a convincing slack fold that explains all of the change in the head/shoulder relationship. I am not asserting a measured head-rule failure: the collapse class skips that automated rule, and visual identity remains the controlling check.
- **Facing: acceptable rightward read.** The opening and hood projection point toward screen right, though the two broadly displayed wings retain a frontal presentation consistent with the identity reference.
- **Unsupported death-collapse read: fails.** Both wings retain prominent raised outer joints and long, extended upper spars from shoulder toward tip. The membrane panels remain broad, spread fans with organized radial tension. Outer tips and torn hems droop, but the main wing structure still looks held out. This is compatible with a lowered flight stroke or tired hover. It does not distinctly establish two unsupported, slack wings or loss of lift.
- **Hood slackening: insufficient.** The hood remains a high, rounded, structured cap above a comparatively upright neck/body axis. Its opening still has a firm arch. The reference's angular hood has become fuller rather than visibly buckled and hanging. Downward cloth tails alone do not supply the missing collapse cue.

## Smallest correction

Keep the existing painted creature, attached wing roots, materials, and full silhouette. Repose both wings from the shoulders so the long spars and outer joints hang substantially downward rather than maintaining the broad supporting cross. Add genuine elbow/wrist buckling and overlapping, loose membrane folds while preserving the reference's physical wing lengths. Buckle the hood forward/down at its existing neck attachment and let its opening crumple/slacken; retain the identity hood's physical scale and a readable rightward opening. The silhouette should show a loss of support at first glance, without relying on torn hems, particles, or a later animation frame to explain it. No ash or debris is needed for this key.

ART_CONTRACT §9 makes intended-action readability a visual gate even when normalization and size bands are satisfied. This key retains much of the identity, but the central proposed pose requirement is not yet met.
