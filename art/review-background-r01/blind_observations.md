# Blind observations

Recorded after viewing lineup_background.png and each of the four individual strip images, before reading intent, metadata, or audit.

- bg_sky: Square field of dark purple storm clouds. Mottled painted texture with scattered lavender openings; no moon, figures, or hard objects.
- bg_distant_silhouette: Very wide band of blue teal mist and many Gothic castle towers, bridges and spires beneath purple clouds. Several depths of tiny structures. Left and right halves visibly echo each other around the centre.
- bg_midground_arch: Wide dark grey ruined Gothic wall with open pointed arches, broken towers, and stone tracery. Blue distant castles and purple clouds show through and above the wall. The large broken towers and arches repeat in reflected pairs, with a narrow central pillar. This looks like a complete opaque scenic painting rather than isolated architectural cutouts.
- bg_foreground_frame: Almost entirely empty transparent checkerboard, with small black bare branches entering from the two upper corners. No lower framing or central obstruction.

The lineup shows the cloud panel, distant city band, closer stone architecture band, and sparse corner branches as a coherent cold dark Gothic family. The architecture is considerably sharper and darker than the distant city.
