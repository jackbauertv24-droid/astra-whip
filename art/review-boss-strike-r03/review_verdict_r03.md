## Anatomy, scale, style and measurement adjudication

The low recovery poses bend hips/knees rather than uniformly shrinking the character. The helmet retains its physical dimensions and crown/face construction in the native files; shoulder plates, gauntlet and boots remain the same objects. Both legs are traceable under the shroud, including through the deepest pose. The small backward-pointing shape beside the forward ankle is a rigid armor/heel overlap: it is angular and somewhat stiff at native scale, but it remains attached to the lower leg and does not read as a floating fragment, extra foot or missing ankle in the runtime images. This is polish debt, not a release-blocking anatomy defect.

The oversized arm emerges from the forward clavicle beneath the retained shoulder construction. Its raised upper-arm contour is relatively straight and its early motion feels rigged; it does not detach, change length, sprout another limb or change material. The held fist supplies the silhouette warning without depending on color. The cold corroded iron and muted decayed cloth fit the painted gothic style references; no gore, glowing weapon, warm decorative cue, baked floor shadow or visible opaque rectangle is introduced.

The scoped contract labels supplemental recovery hurt variants `strike`, retains a narrow 0.92–1.03 standing-height band and all lower-body/size/pivot gates, and skips the top-band head proxy. These are supplemental poses of the grounded strike-recovery action, not additional mandatory inventory or a replacement for base standing hurt. This local classification is acceptable for this pack because I directly checked all nine helmets at native resolution against the standing source: the head is translated and counter-rotated without shrinking. It does **not** authorize ignoring head identity in other clips, nor does this review amend the pinned mandatory contract. A general proportional top-band head check alone cannot establish physical head size in a stoop, because its sampling band changes with total figure height.

Audit reports 0 DEFECT and 0 FLAG across these nine scoped clips; there are no numeric flags to waive. That result was not used as a substitute for visual judgment. The overhead height exception is already explicit in the mandatory windup definition; the raised arm is not normalized down to standing-body height. The retained generated overhead key is visibly a distinct oversized construction and was not silently substituted into the delivered sequence.
