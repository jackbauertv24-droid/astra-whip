# Blind observations

## idle f0
Upright shrouded figure with one pale arm hanging down. Tall capped stone and rooted plinth remain behind the body.

## idle f1
Arm lifts slightly outward at wrist and elbow. Hood and long layered drapery keep the same narrow silhouette.

## idle f2
Hanging hand shifts outward a little more. Head remains hidden in the olive hood.

## idle f3
Arm reaches its widest relaxed offset. Rooted lower robe stays fixed on the stone base.

## idle f4
Hand draws inward toward the original hanging position. Shoulder and hood barely change.

## idle f5
Hand returns close to the initial position. The pose reads as a subtle breathing or swaying loop.

## hurt f0
Pale arm folds across chest with hand near the upper robe. Hood leans slightly forward beside the fixed stone.

## hurt f1
Folded arm holds near the chest with a small settling shift. Figure stays tall and balanced on the plinth.

## hurt f2
Arm hangs again beside the robe. Overall silhouette resembles the upright resting figure.

## death f0
Arm is folded across the chest and the figure remains tall. The silhouette resembles the first hurt pose.

## death f1
Hood drops forward and the arm hangs down. Stone cap and ground base appear fixed while upper body slumps.

## death f2
Head and shoulders sink farther forward with the hand hanging low. Long robe still supports a narrow upright lower half.

## death f3
Slump holds with hand hanging low and hood bent. The pose reads limp although most of the robe remains standing.

## death f4
Whole figure and stone become faint, retaining the previous slumped outline. Little visible breakup occurs inside the silhouette.

## death f5
Low pale ash mound replaces the figure and upright stone. A few detached flecks rise above the mound.
