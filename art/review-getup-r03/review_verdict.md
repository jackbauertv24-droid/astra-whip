## Contract comparison and native-frame review

Scope is the two supplied clips only. I opened all seven native PNGs, the complete onion sheet and all five registered style-reference images. Verdicts concern the delivered images; unrelated missing clips are outside this review. No art was edited.

Both clips face right. The neutral pose is a complete human from head to boot soles, reaches the standing-height guide, and matches the registered neutral reference. Across recovery poses the tied-back hair, face profile, mantle, cream sleeves, leather bracers, long coat and hip coil remain recognisable. The head remains attached through a compact neck; I find no stretched-neck join, missing limb, cut-off edge, detached component, key rectangle or obvious debris. Low poses fold hips and knees rather than shrink the standing silhouette. Small differences in visible coat area and foreshortening do not independently establish a physical-scale defect.

Adjacent-pair inspection:

- F0→F1: the torso rolls from elbow support to a planted rear palm while the extended leg draws into a seated base. Large silhouette-area loss is explained by folding/overlap of legs and coat, with no comparable reduction of head or boot scale. This is a supported recovery action.
- F1→F2: rear palm remains down, forward boot remains in approximately the same footprint, and torso inclines over the folded legs. The motion is coherent.
- F2→F3: the rear support hand releases toward the hip coil while the other hand reaches forward to the ground beside the boot. The planted forward foot and folded rear knee provide a base during this hand transfer. The change is brisk at 75 ms but anatomically readable; no arm disappears or changes length.
- F3→F4: **defect**. The loaded forward boot retreats from roughly x=300–348 to x=256–296 in the native canvas (about 45–50 px), while the torso rises and the bracing forward palm leaves the floor. Both endpoints show the boot planted. There is no visible lifted-foot/replant phase or retained palm support for that repositioning. In the onion the orange shin/sole jump behind the blue support footprint, so the body appears to slide its supporting leg backward as it pushes upward. This breaks the weight/foot continuity of the otherwise supported sequence.
- F4→F5: the forward boot remains at approximately x=256–296, the rear foot comes into the standing base, and the torso straightens. The rise is large but follows the bent-leg pose. This pair does not independently fail.

The reused clip reads as getting up rather than falling backwards. Its floor roll, seated preparation, hand brace and upward push are clear. The support-foot discontinuity above still blocks acceptance under A-11 and §9.
