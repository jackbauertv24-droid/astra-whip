Scope: `art/production-candidates/swooper-death-limp-r04/key.png` only as a proposed death-collapse key, under ART_CONTRACT §9 and the declared D23 derivation. This reviewer did not make the art. The candidate alone was viewed first and its plain description saved to `blind_observations.md` before opening identity, specification, or derivation files. No previous rejected review or builder verdict was inspected.

References inspected: the approved `swooper-identity-r02/key.png`; all five images listed by `art/style-lock.json`; `docs/ART_CONTRACT.md`, `docs/ART_BIBLE.md`, `docs/ASSET_BRIEFS.md`, `contract/art_contract.json`; and the candidate's `derivation.json`. ART_BIBLE and ASSET_BRIEFS are located under docs in this repository.

## Findings

- **Death-collapse pose — FAIL.** The wings have moved down relative to the identity's wide flight cross, but their internal articulation still reads as held open. The image-left wing retains a high elbow/leading-edge arch with a broad tensioned membrane fan extending sideways before its tips descend. The image-right wing likewise retains an outward bent elbow and open fan, with clearly spread struts. These are wings in a lowered beat or crouched flight configuration; they do not convincingly read as slack appendages after loss of support. The blind observation independently identified this supported-wing read. Rotating whole spread wing layers downward is a declared permissible method, but the resulting pose does not satisfy the intended action under §11.
- **Attached complete anatomy — acceptable at this scale.** Both wings, hood, and trailing shroud remain present and visually connected to the central figure. No boots or human legs are required for this explicitly legless flight actor. I see no severed parts or gore.
- **Hood and facing — acceptable.** The hood has a forward/downward inclination and still presents a rightward pointed opening/profile. This adds some subdued acting, although it does not overcome the supported wings.
- **Identity, scale, and material — acceptable visually.** The ragged gray-brown shroud, dark hood interior, membrane texture, wing proportions, and paint treatment remain recognizably the approved creature. The smaller horizontal envelope is explained by articulation rather than an obvious whole-character shrink. The declared scale factor of 1 is consistent with the visible preservation of painted features. This is visual assessment of one key, not an automated per-frame scale audit.
- **Visible processing defects — no blocking defect observed.** The silhouette has comfortable margins, and I see no hard canvas crop, separate debris, conspicuous key-color fringe, or large rectangular extraction scar. Shoulder layering is crowded but does not clearly detach. No technical alpha or component audit is claimed here.

## Required correction

Replace the held-open wing fans with visibly relaxed articulation: lower the shoulder-to-elbow support, let the elbow/wrist and long struts hang under gravity, and bring the membrane sections into a narrower drooping or partially folded shape with slack folds. In particular remove the tall image-left supported arch and the image-right lateral open fan. Preserve both full attached wings, original segment lengths and painted material, the drooped right-facing hood, and the current clean margins. A simple additional rotation of the same rigid spread fans is insufficient unless the resulting silhouette actually reads as limp.

This verdict concerns the static key alone. Full animation, collapse progression, temporal consistency, timing, alpha checks, and ash-settle remain separate approval gates.

STATIC KEY REVIEW: FAIL
