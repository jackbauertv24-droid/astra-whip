# Independent static key review — swooper death collapse r01

Scope: one painted collapse key, `art/production-candidates/swooper-death-collapse-r01/key.png`. This is not animation, timing, continuity, full clip, or release approval. Reviewer did not create or edit this art and received no builder verdict. Blind observations were saved before reading the intent, references, and contracts.

Reviewed the approved identity r02 and recovery-catch r01 keys, all five image references listed in `art/style-lock.json`, `docs/ART_CONTRACT.md`, `docs/ART_BIBLE.md`, `docs/ASSET_BRIEFS.md`, and `contract/art_contract.json`.

## Findings

- **Full creature / identity: PASS.** Hooded head, narrow shrouded torso, long tattered lower drapery, and two complete membrane wings are present. The small concealed head, slender wing supports, torn membrane, and layered shroud remain recognizably the approved creature. No added legs, extra wings, exposed wound, or severed part appears.
- **Material / style: PASS.** Muted ash and old-bone gray-brown painted surfaces, restrained highlights, leathery membrane, and matte worn shroud remain consistent with the identity/recovery keys and the locked gothic painted style. No gore or new luminous effect.
- **Anatomy / apparent scale: PASS visually.** Head, torso, wing fingers, and hanging shroud retain plausible reference proportions. The body is not visibly shrunk to manufacture collapse. Apparent limb and torso scale is stable under the pose change. This is visual judgment, not a numerical size-band audit.
- **Attachment: PASS.** Both wing roots connect visibly to the shoulder/upper-body structure. Nothing reads detached or floating.
- **Limp wings / loss of flight: FAIL.** The image-left wing rises above the head to a high, sharply supported elbow/wrist and spreads a broad, relatively taut membrane fan outward. Its strong upper-left arch still reads as an active raised flight wing. The image-right wing hangs lower, but its angular raised wrist and open fan also retain a supported flight shape. Against the approved cruise-like identity, this reads primarily as an asymmetric wingbeat with a drooping body, rather than an unmistakable failure of lift. The head remains held approximately level and projected forward, reinforcing the active flight ambiguity. A single collapse key need not be a final settled corpse, but it must communicate loss of muscular support clearly enough to distinguish it from flight or recovery without animation context.
- **Facing / isolation: PASS visually.** Hood opening points right. No scenery, floor shadow, backdrop object, loose debris, or edge crop is visible. The complete silhouette has generous canvas clearance. Transparency is displayed as black by the viewer; no numerical alpha/debris certification is claimed here.

## Required correction

Repaint the wing joints into a visibly unsupported fall: lower or fold the high image-left wing at its shoulder and wrist so it no longer makes a raised load-bearing flight arch; let the outer finger supports and membrane tips sag downward with slack folds. Relax the image-right wing similarly so the pair reads limp while both roots remain attached. Add a small clear slackening of head/neck or torso pitch to reinforce the lost-lift state. Preserve the approved head and torso scale, wing lengths, membrane material, right-facing identity, full silhouette, and clean isolation. Do not solve this by shrinking the creature, cutting off wings, adding debris, or adding gore.

A new independent reviewer should review the corrected static key. Full animation approval still requires the separate clip review mandated by ART_CONTRACT §9.
