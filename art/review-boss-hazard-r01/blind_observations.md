# Blind visual observations
Observed lineup and every strip only, before documentation or intent.

Lineup: all ten tiles show the same thin crowned, ragged armored giant. Head shape and material appear consistent. Hazard recover and stooped hurt tiles are visibly lowered and tilted; feet stay on baseline. Long hanging armored hand is large but consistent across poses. No obvious detached parts in lineup.

Windup: F0 standing with hanging fist. F1 forearm lifts diagonally and closed hand moves outward. F2 opens fingers while arm rises. F3 extends open claw near horizontal. F4 slightly higher/longer reach. F5 holds reach with slight chest shift. F6 lowers reach a little. F7 holds slightly lowered open reach. Feet stay planted; head remains recognizably same size. Motion is restrained, almost entirely one arm, rather than large torso action.

Execute: F0 extended open arm matches end of windup. F1 forearm descends diagonally. F2 open hand hangs low with wrist bent; elbow connection has a sharp angular silhouette. F3 fist closes and torso/head lower into lean. No independent striking contact is obvious in strip alone.

Recover: F0 low leaning body with fist beside knee. F1 similar lean with small cloth change. F2 still low, head near same level. F3 starts raising torso and hand. F4 higher torso and head. F5 upright with hanging fist. Ground contact maintained. Crown/head appears same physical object tilted, not a replacement. Cloth changes can look like layers sliding together.

Hurt hazard late: F0 slightly lowered/leaned body; F1 rises slightly; F2 almost upright. All three retain joined arm and closed fist, planted feet and consistent crown. Difference is small, primarily torso tilt.

Hurt: F0 upright; F1 small lean/back shift; F2 returns toward upright. Head, hands and feet stable.
Hurt half stoop: F0 mild lean; F1 marginal lean/back shift; F2 rises. Same crown and huge gauntlet retained.
Hurt rising: F0 bent body; F1 slight shift; F2 raises shoulders and head a little.
Hurt stooped: F0 low head and bent body; F1 similarly low; F2 slight lift. Hand remains near knee.
Idle: F0 upright; F1 slight body shift; F2 subtle torso lift; F3 subtle maximum lift; F4 similar lifted pose; F5 slight descent; F6 descends; F7 near starting pose. Minimal motion, feet fixed.
Walk: F0 spread grounded feet; F1 rear leg starts lift; F2 rear foot high and folded; F3 crosses toward front; F4 opposite foot becomes rear; F5 stride opens; F6 opposite foot lifted; F7 foot held forward before return. Torso largely rigid. Some leg/cloth overlap is dense and a lifted boot can read as a loose wedge, but no plainly extra limb.

These are observations only, not acceptance conclusions.
