## Cross-piece observations

- **Anatomy and identity:** Both hero poses show the whole body, including two legs and boots; neither is a stretched torso. The face, tied dark hair, cream sleeves, shoulder garment, split coat, bracers, boots and hip equipment remain recognizably consistent. The extended pose does not read as a smaller character. Both face right. No crouch or transition is supplied or approved.
- **Pursuer anatomy:** Four limbs are visible as two supporting hind limbs and two raised elongated forelimbs. In this alert pose the creature can read momentarily as a stooped humanoid, but its wrong proportions and rear-up silhouette are consistent with the specified quadruped tell. The patrol contrast can only be described at this gate, not tested from a nonexistent patrol frame.
- **Sockets and whip:** The attack hand is visible and the cyan mark sits against its lower edge. This is body-only calibration, so absence of a painted extended whip is expected and is not a defect. The schematic trajectory's failure to pass through the yellow region is the relevant concern here. The neutral hip coil is compact; grip construction and socket relationship need the omitted overlay.
- **Readability:** Hero and platform survive the supplied 844×475 phone/grayscale views. Facial features are necessarily small, but the head retains a human profile/light face read. The environment is busy around the play plane without completely swallowing the hero. Equivalent attack and enemy evidence is not present; approval cannot be extrapolated to them.
- **Terrain:** At the horizontal joins in `terrain_repeat.png`, mortar divisions and the top ledge continue plausibly. Cracks and carving do not protrude into the walkable silhouette. A repeated motif alone is not a seam failure.
- **Style, originality and horror:** All five use compatible painted matte materials, restrained lighting and gothic decay. I see no recognizable franchise crest/costume copy, explicit blood, wounds, exposed organs, dismemberment, photoreal/illustrated mismatch, energy whip, cute-animal treatment or baked text in the artwork. This is a visual assessment of the supplied images, not external provenance verification.
- **Evidence boundary:** The missing overlays/previews are failures of required calibration evidence, not claims that the unseen tests would necessarily fail. They should be supplied without gratuitously regenerating otherwise acceptable art. A fresh independent reviewer must assess the completed pack after correction.
